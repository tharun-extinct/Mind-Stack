A VPN provides full encryption and system-wide privacy, while a proxy only masks your IP for specific apps or browsers without encrypting your traffic.
Key Differences
1. Functionality and Coverage
VPN: Encrypts all internet traffic from your device and routes it through a secure server, protecting your data system-wide and hiding your IP address from ISPs, hackers, and websites. 

Proxy: Acts as an intermediary for specific applications or browsers, masking your IP address but usually does not encrypt traffic, leaving your data potentially visible. 


2. Security and Privacy
VPN: Offers strong encryption (e.g., AES-256), secure tunnels, and protection on public Wi-Fi networks. It ensures privacy and anonymity for all online activities. 

Proxy: Provides minimal security. It can hide your IP but does not protect your data from interception, making it less safe on public networks. 


3. Speed and Performance
VPN: May slightly reduce internet speed due to encryption overhead, but modern protocols like WireGuard minimize this impact. 

Proxy: Often faster than VPNs because it does not encrypt traffic, but this comes at the cost of security. 


4. Use Cases
VPN: Ideal for securing sensitive data, bypassing censorship, accessing geo-restricted content, and maintaining privacy on public networks. 

Proxy: Suitable for simple tasks like temporarily accessing geo-restricted websites or masking your IP for a single browser session. 


5. Organizational Applications
VPN: Can be client-based or site-to-site, connecting remote users securely to corporate networks with encrypted tunnels. 

Proxy: Often used in enterprises for traffic filtering, access control, and anonymizing requests, but without full encryption.

Summary
In essence, VPNs are safer and more comprehensive, providing encryption, privacy, and system-wide protection. Proxies are lighter and faster but only mask your IP for specific apps or browsers and do not secure your data. For real privacy, especially on public Wi-Fi or when handling sensitive information, a VPN is the recommended choice.
