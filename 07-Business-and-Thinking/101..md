TAN (Tax Deduction and Collection Account Number) is a mandatory 10-digit alphanumeric identifier for businesses and individuals in India responsible for deducting or collecting tax at source.
What is TAN?
TAN stands for Tax Deduction and Collection Account Number. It is a unique 10-character alphanumeric code issued by the Income Tax Department of India under Section 203A of the Income Tax Act, 1961. TAN is required for entities that deduct TDS (Tax Deducted at Source) or collect TCS (Tax Collected at Source) on payments such as salaries, rent, professional fees, interest, or contractor payments. The format typically includes four letters, five numbers, and a final letter (e.g., DELH12345A), with the first three letters representing the jurisdiction code.

How to Apply for TAN
TAN can be applied online via the NSDL-PROTEAN portal or offline using Form 49B. The process involves submitting business details, PAN of the responsible person, and payment of the application fee. TAN is typically issued within 7–15 working days.
