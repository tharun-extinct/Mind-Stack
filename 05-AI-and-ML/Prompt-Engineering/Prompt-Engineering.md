Prompt Modifiers -> 


Tone Modifiers
Shapes the emotional and interpersonal dynamics of the Prompt, influencing how the Al
frames its response

. Polite: 'Could you politely explain the process of photosynthesis?'

. Casual: 'Hey, can you tell me in a casual tone what causes rain?'

. Professional: 'Please provide a detailed analysis of the French Revolution. Maintain a
professional tone.'

. Friendly: 'How does a rocket work? Keep it friendly.'

. Sarcastic: 'Help me understand Schrödinger's cat? Use sarcasm.'

. Encouraging: 'Could you explain Einstein's theory of relativity? Be encouraging.'

---

 Style Modifiers
{
Dictates the formal or informal nature of the response, enriching the text with a specific
stylistic quality

. Formal: 'It is requested that you elucidate the principles behind nuclear fusion. Use formal
language.'

. Informal: 'Wanna explain why we have leap years? Keep it informal.'

. Humorous: 'Tell me a humorous joke about gravity.'

. Literary: 'Imagine a world where water is scarce. Describe it in a literary style.'

. Concise: 'In brief, explain how airplanes stay in the air.'

. Verbose: 'I'd appreciate a thorough and detailed explanation of how the internet works.'

}
---

Format => 
Specifies the organizational structure of the Al's response, ideal for clarity and specific
informational needs


Bullet points, dialogue, Essay, summary, Q/A

, Perspective
{

Guides the Al to adopt a specific viewpoint or narrative voice, enhancing the depth and
relevance of the response


First Person: 'As a manager, I would like to understand risk management. Explain from a first-
person perspective.'

Second Person: 'You are walking through a forest and find a lost kitten. Describe the scene in
second person.'

Third Person: 'Describe how John would solve a conflict at work. Use third person narration.'

Objective: 'Objectively analyze the causes of the American Civil War. Remain objective.'

Subjective: 'Share your personal thoughts on the importance of art education.
Be subjective.'
}

, Complexity

{

Adjusts the level of detail and technical specificity, tailoring the response to the expertise of
the audience

. Simple: 'Explain global warming in simple terms.'

. Complex: 'Delve into the complexities of artificial neural networks.'

. Technical: 'Provide a technical description of blockchain technology.'

. Layman's Terms: 'Explain quantum computing like I'm 5 years old.'

. Advanced: 'Discuss the implications of quantum entanglement for an expert audience.'
}
, Purpose{

Defines the intent behind the query, directing the Al to construct its response accordingly

They are:

. Instructional: 'Instruct how to set up a saltwater aquarium.'

. Exploratory: 'Explore the reasons why Rome fell.'

. Argumentative: 'Argue in favor of renewable energy sources.'

. Descriptive: 'Describe the scene at a bustling market.'

. Comparative: 'Compare and contrast socialism and capitalism.'

---

}
, Audience {

Ensures that the content is appropriate for the intended listeners or readers, making it
accessible and engaging

They are:

. For Beginners: 'Explain the theory of relativity to a beginner.'

. For Experts: 'Discuss the latest advancements in CRISPR technology for experts.'

. For Children: 'How would you explain the Internet to a child?'

. For Business Professionals: 'As a business consultant, advise on market entry strategies
for China.'

. For General Public: 'Inform the public about the benefits of vaccination.'

}

Prompt Example - 1

Prompt:
Given the recent developments in renewable energy, write a brief,
persuasive essay targeting policymakers on the advantages of solar
energy investment. The essay should be formal, include at least
three benefits, and conclude with a call to action.


Instruction - `write a brief, persuasive ` - Establishes the task and the format expected in the response (purpose)


Context - `Given the recent developments in renewable energy` - Ensures the AI incorporates recent data


Specificity - `on the advantages of solar
energy investment` - Sharpens the model's focus, enhancing content relevance


Tone & Style - The essay should be formal - Aligns the language and presentation with the expectations of the target audience


Output Format - include at least
three benefits, and conclude with a call to action. - Organizes the content to facilitate
clarity and persuasion, guiding the
reader through logical argumentation
to a proactive conclusion

Prompt Priming


· Involves preparing the Al model with specific information or context before presenting the main task


. Pre-conditioning steers the model's responses to be
more aligned with the desired outcome


Types,
- Topic-Specific Prompting 
- Context-Specific Prompting
- Opinion Prompting
- Tone & Style Prompting

---

Topic Specific Prompting:
Prompt: Considering recent advancements, discuss the impact of artificial intelligence in healthcare

Priming Strategy: 
· Prime the Al with the Prompt,
'What are the recent advancements in artificial intelligence?'
. Feed it a summary of a recent article on Al developments


Purpose:
· Sets the stage by refreshing the Al's "memory" of the latest Al technologies
· Focuses subsequent analyses specifically on applications in healthcare


---


Tone & Style Prompting:

Prompt: Write a humorous blog post
about the challenges of working
from home


Priming Strategy

Feed the Al examples of humorous
writing on workplace dynamics to
set the desired tone, such as
excerpts from satirical articles


Purpose

Encourage the Al to replicate a
similar humorous style, effectively
capturing the intended vibe of
the blog post


---

Context-Specific Prompting

Incorporates detailed situational or background information to
produce responses that reflect a deeper understanding of the context.



Prompt

As an expert in tropical diseases,
discuss the potential threats of
climate change on the spread of
malaria in South-East Asia



Priming Strategy

Providing the Al with a
background article on malaria
and climate change impacts in
South-East Asia before issuing the
main Prompt


Purpose

Primes the Al with relevant
geographic and scientific
context, ensuring that its analysis
is appropriately detailed and
region-specific

. Effective Prompt priming is akin to providing a
lens through which the Al views the query

. This technique sharpens the Al's focus and
ensures the responses are substantially more
tailored and relevant

. Significantly enhance the Al's performance
across various applications Eg: academic
research to content creation
