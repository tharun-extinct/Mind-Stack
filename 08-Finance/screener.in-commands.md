## Screener.in commands


#### Multi bagger

current price < Book Value                          -> Under valued
Debt to equity < 1 AND
Return on equity > 15 AND
Return on assets > 5




#### High Dividend Yeild Stock

Dividend yield > 4 AND
Dividend last year > Average 5years dividend AND
Market Capitalization > 10000 AND
Current price < Intrinsic Value AND
Debt to equity < 1 AND
Return on equity > 15 AND
Return on assets > 5 AND
Price to Earning < Industry PE





#### Undervalued stocks 

Current price < 200 AND
Current price < Intrinsic Value AND

Altman Z Score > 3 AND
Debt to equity <1 AND
Return on equity > 15 AND
Return on assets > 5 AND
Profit growth 5Years > 10 AND
Market Capitalization > 5000
